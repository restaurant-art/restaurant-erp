UVPRO Privacy policy

WhatsApp receipt data deletion

Vestano Retail Pvt Ltd is responsible for UVPRO. Use these instructions to request deletion of information processed through UVPRO’s WhatsApp receipts integration.

Request deletion

  1. Email info@vestanoretail.com with the subject UVPRO WhatsApp data deletion.
  2. If you received a receipt, include the store name and bill number or purchase date. If you administer a connected store, include the store name and the WhatsApp Business Account ID, if known.
  3. Describe the data you want removed. Keep passwords, verification codes, access tokens, payment card details, and full receipt links out of the email.

Vestano Retail Pvt Ltd may need to verify your request or refer it to the store that issued the bill. Some sales records may need to be kept for tax, accounting, or other applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Disconnect a store

In UVPRO, a store owner or administrator can go to Settings → Meta WhatsApp Integration → Disconnect and confirm. This removes the stored Meta access token, turns off automatic sending, and cancels queued receipts. To revoke the app’s access on Meta as well, remove the integration in Meta’s business settings.

Use the deletion request above for information that remains in receipt or delivery history after you disconnect.

Stop future WhatsApp receipts

Tell the store that you no longer want WhatsApp receipts. You can still ask for another receipt option when you pay.
