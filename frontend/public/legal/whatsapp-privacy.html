WhatsApp receipts privacy policy

Vestano Retail Pvt Ltd is responsible for UVPRO. This policy explains how the company handles information in the UVPRO WhatsApp receipts integration.

When a store connects WhatsApp

A store administrator signs in to Meta and chooses which WhatsApp Business Account and phone number to connect. Meta shares the authorized business and account identifiers, the sending number and its display name, and information about the store’s receipt templates. UVPRO keeps an encrypted access token so the store can use the connection after setup.

When a store sends a receipt

When you agree to receive a receipt on WhatsApp, the store records your phone number and consent at checkout. UVPRO uses that number and the saved bill to send the receipt from the store’s WhatsApp number. Depending on the approved template, the message may include the store name, bill number, total, a receipt link, items, taxes, discounts, or payment method. The integration is used for receipts and authorized test messages.

UVPRO records delivery information from Meta, such as whether a receipt was sent, delivered, read, or failed. Authorized store staff use this history to check delivery and resolve problems.

Who receives the information

Meta processes the recipient’s phone number, message content, and delivery information to provide the WhatsApp Business Platform. Amazon Web Services provides the infrastructure used to run and store UVPRO data. The store that issued the bill can access its receipts and message history through UVPRO.

Receipt links and saved records

Digital receipt links expire 30 days after they are issued. Anyone who has the link can view the receipt while it is active. The receipt page omits the customer’s contact details.

Expiry ends access through the link. The store’s billing records and UVPRO’s receipt and delivery history may remain available for billing, reconciliation, support, or applicable recordkeeping requirements. Disconnecting the integration removes the store’s saved access token and cancels queued receipts.

Your choices and requests

You can decline WhatsApp delivery at checkout or ask the store to stop sending future receipts through WhatsApp. For access, correction, or deletion requests about this integration, see the data deletion and contact instructions.

Contact Vestano Retail Pvt Ltd at info@vestanoretail.com for privacy questions or requests.
